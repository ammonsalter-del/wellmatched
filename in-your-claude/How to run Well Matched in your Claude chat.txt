HOW TO RUN WELL MATCHED IN YOUR OWN CLAUDE CHAT

Well Matched, developed by Ammon Salter, Warwick Business School.
Free and open source under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 licence (CC BY-NC 4.0).

This version needs no API key. It runs in your own Claude chat, on your own Claude account, and uses your plan's allowance. Nothing goes to the developer.

If you have an Anthropic API key, use the website version instead. It finds each department's staff for you, which this version cannot.


BEFORE YOU START

Have these ready, as PDF or Word files or as text:
  - your CV
  - your cover letter
  - your job market paper
  - for each department: the job advert, if there is one

You need a Claude account on claude.ai, in a web browser or the Claude app.


1. OPEN THE TOOL

  a. Start a new chat in Claude.
  b. Attach the file well-matched-chat.html.
  c. Copy this message and send it:

     Please publish the attached file exactly as it is, as an HTML artifact, so I can use it here. Do not change, shorten or rewrite any of it.

  d. The tool opens beside the chat. You will see the Well Matched logo and the heading "Running in your own Claude chat".

Keep this chat open until you have finished. If you close it, what you typed is lost.


2. FILL IN "YOUR MATERIALS AND PREFERENCES"

  a. Upload or paste your CV, your cover letter and your job market paper.
  b. Answer the questions below them: when you started and will finish your PhD, your supervisors, the countries you would move to, and the rest.
  c. For your supervisors, give each one's name, position and where they publish, or paste their profile pages.


3. FIND EACH DEPARTMENT'S STAFF, IN THE CHAT

The tool cannot search the web, so you do this part in the chat, once for each department.

  a. In the tool, under "The departments you are considering", press "Copy the message".
  b. Click in the chat box (outside the tool) and paste the message.
  c. At the end of the message, replace the two parts in square brackets:
       [your topics]                 with your research topics, in a few words
       [school and department]       with the school and department, for example
                                     "Copenhagen Business School, Department of Strategy and Innovation"
  d. Send it. Claude searches the school's website and replies with a list of staff.
  e. Read the list. Delete anyone or anything that looks wrong.
  f. Copy the list.

Repeat a to f for each department. You can send the message again with a different department each time.


4. FILL IN "THE DEPARTMENTS YOU ARE CONSIDERING"

For each department:
  a. In "School, department and country", type its name.
  b. In "The job advert", paste the advert if there is one.
  c. In "The department's people", paste the staff list you copied in Step 3.
  d. Fill in the other boxes if you can: the start date, and anyone there who knows you.
  e. Press "Add another department" for the next one.

A department with nothing pasted in "The department's people" gets only a thin reading.


5. READ YOUR MATCH

  a. Under "Read it as a committee would", press "Read my match".
  b. Wait. It takes a few minutes, longer with more departments. Keep the chat open.
  c. Your results appear under "Your matches". Select any square for the reasons behind it.


6. KEEP A COPY

  a. Press "Save a report (PDF)".
  b. In the window that opens, press "Print or save as PDF", then choose "Save as PDF".
  The report has your results and all the material used.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  - Claude rewrites or shortens the file, or says it is too long:
    start a new chat and try Step 1 again. If it still fails, ask the person who gave you the tool for the smaller copy.
  - The tool says Claude is not available here:
    your chat cannot run tools like this one. Use the website version with an API key.
  - A message says your plan is at its usage limit:
    wait, and press "Read my match" again later.
  - A department "could not be read this time":
    press "Read my match" again.
  - The staff list from Step 3 looks wrong or empty:
    find the department's staff page on the school's website yourself and paste it into "The department's people".
